## **Introduction

**Data Jobs Dashboard V2** is an executive-level, single-page control center engineered for **Job Seekers, Data Analysts, and Hiring Managers** who need concise, high-impact market intelligence. While Version 1 provided a detailed multi-page exploration, **Version 2 condenses complex job market dynamics into an optimized, high-contrast Dark Theme interface**. 

Powered by a global 2024 data science job postings dataset, V2 empowers decision-makers to effortlessly analyze global hiring trends, skill penetration rates, and salary distribution parameters without navigating across multiple pages.

## **Skills Showcased**

- ⚙️ **Data Engineering** (ETL): Used **Power Query** to handle missing values, standardize data types, and organize datasets for modeling in order to clean and transform raw job posting data.
- 🧮 **DAX Analytics**: Developed dynamic DAX measures (using `Median` ) to provide unambiguous financial insights free from outlier distortion, as well as dynamic hiring channel estimates.
- 📊 **Core Visual Analytics**: To assess job distributions, monitor hiring patterns over time, and compare pay structures across positions, area plots, trend lines, and horizontal and vertical bar charts were used.
- 🗺️ **Geospatial Analysis**: Interactive map charts were set up to show the distribution of jobs worldwide and pinpoint regional hiring density in various nations.
- 🔢 **KPI Indicators** & Detailed Tables: Combined interactive, sortable data tables for granular information with small KPI summary cards for important metrics (such `Median Yearly Salary` and `Job Count`)
- 🖱️**Interactive Reporting** 
     -   **Slicers:** To dynamically filter the report by Job Title.
    -   **Buttons & Bookmarks:** For streamlined navigation and managing report views (including Drill-Through).
    

### **Dashboard Overview (Version 2.0 - Single Page Focus)**
This second iteration consolidates the dashboard into a single, focused page, designed to give job seekers the most critical market insights at their fingertips.

![Data Jobs Dashboard v2.0](/Data%20jobs%20V2/images/Data%20jobs%20Dashboard%20v2.png)

This page acts as your concise mission control for the data job market. It showcases key performance indicators (KPIs) like **Job Count, Skills Per Job, Median Yearly Salary, and Median Hourly Salary**. You can also quickly see **Skill Popularity** (by job percent or count) and compare **Salaries across different Job Titles**, all designed for an efficient overview.


## Conclusion

This updated dashboard (Version 2.0) showcases Power BI's ability to transform extensive job posting data into a powerful, streamlined tool for career analysis. It empowers **Job Seekers, Job Transitioners, & Job Swappers** by allowing them to filter and explore essential market insights efficiently on a single page, helping them make informed decisions about their next career move.
